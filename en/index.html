Core modules

One workbench for five media types

Each module follows the real workflow: import sources, search, play or read, and keep your place.

Video

Multi-source CMS and Bilibili sources, cross-source search, HLS / FLV / MP4 playback, history, and favorites.

  • Cross-source search
  • Ad filtering
  • Downloads and cache

Music

Browse charts, playlists, artists, and albums with local play history and lyrics.

  • Charts
  • Playlists
  • Lyrics

Novels

Import rule-based book sources, maintain a bookshelf and history, and export TXT / EPUB / HTML.

  • Rule sources
  • Reading progress
  • Export

Comics

Browse categories and rankings, build a bookshelf, and read with scroll or paged desktop modes.

  • Chapter progress
  • Offline cache
  • Reading modes

Live

Load IPTV M3U subscriptions, group channels, and navigate XMLTV guides with EPG positioning.

  • M3U
  • EPG
  • Channel groups
Four platforms

One codebase, four shapes
